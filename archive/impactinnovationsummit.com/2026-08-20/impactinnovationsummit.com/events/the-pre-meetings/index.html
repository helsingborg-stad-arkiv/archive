The Pre-Meetings

September 17, 2024 13:00-15:30 Various locations

Leading up to the meet-up at Helsingborg Arena, many of our co-creators will host pre-meetings where participants can deep-dive into topics related to their work. This is the first chance to meet and hang out with summit attendees and other like-minded people.

Pre-meetings open for everyone

The SustainGov Pre-Meeting

September 17, 2024
13.00-15.30
Helsingborg Arena
Directions

SustainGov invites summit participants to an exclusive pre-meeting, with talks on how public sector can drive sustainable system change. The programme is in Swedish.

Boards and the Valuation of Social Impact Pre-Meeting

September 17, 2024
14.00-15.00
Helsingborg Arena
Directions

At this pre-meeting, Boards Impact Forum will address the governance of social impact and the importance of clarifying the social aspects of board governance.

The Better Shared Pre-Meeting

September 17, 2024
14:00-15:30
Helsingborg Arena
Directions

How can we change the world by starting with ourselves? During this session, we will dive into case studies and discuss how we can turn ideas into reality and create true collaboration, focus on leadership, infrastructure, and culture as keys to success, and xplore how to create practical outcomes and effective partnerships. Hosted by Leadership Arts with special guests.

Pre-meetings for invited networks and others

The One-Water Pre-Meeting
How can we integrate water, energy, and waste management in urban development? Hosted by the RecoLab Network, Lund University and the city of Helsingborg with 15 spots open for everyone. Contact amanda.haux@nsva.se for more details.

Circular Energy Pre-Meeting
How can we accelerate the adoption of circular energy systems in urban areas to maximize energy efficiency and significantly reduce carbon emissions? Hosted by Hållbar Utveckling Skåne, Cradle Net, and CSR Skåne. In collaboration with E.On and Alfa Laval. Invite only.

Startup & Investor Pre-Meeting
How can we empower startups and investors to break through the barriers slowing down the adoption of sustainable and circular business models? Hosted by HETCH and Rosholm Dell. Invite only.

The Climate Pre-Meeting
How can we engage citizens in climate neutrality efforts? Hosted by Helsingborg city, Viable Cities, Net Zero Cities, Democratic Cities, and Lund University Open Academy. Invite only.

The 15-minute city Pre-Meeting
The future society will be based out of cities. What could the future local neighborhood could look like if it was driven by citizens and entrepreneurs? Hosted by Ödet, Drevet & Navet i Sege Park. Invite only.

The REAL Pre-Meeting
How can we develop strategies to meet the future of commerce and logistics? Hosted by Center for Retail and Logistics (REAL) at Lund University.

The AI Pre-Meeting
How can we accelerate the use of AI in the public sector? Hosted by Helsingborg city, DigIT Hub and AI Sweden. Invite only.

The Social Transformation Pre-Meeting
How can we explore new methods to combat domestic violence from a systemic perspective? Hosted by Helsingborg city, in collaboration with partners.

Impact Entrepreneurship Pre-Meeting
How can we address the intertwined challenges of entrepreneurship and unemployment? Hosted by Mindpark and E-commerce Park, this pre-meeting will explore innovative solutions through impact entrepreneurship. We aim to combine business acumen with social purpose to create jobs, empower communities, and drive sustainable economic growth.

The MiH and Climate agreement Pre-Meeting
How can we accelerate the transition to self-sufficiency and storage of fossil-free energy? This pre-meeting, co-hosted by the Marketing Association in the Helsingborg region and The Climate Agreement in Helsingborg, targets mid-sized companies within the Helsingborg Family. The session focuses on exploring practical opportunities, challenges, and collaborative strategies, featuring concrete examples from Öresundskraft, Greenfood, and Wihlborgs, who are leading the way in renewable energy initiatives and responsible green transitions.

Innovation Pioneers Pre-Meeting
How can we overcome the challenges faced by change makers in large organizations? This session will tackle issues like approval, funding, and management support. Innovation Pioneers will map these obstacles, explore solutions, and introduce the Banana Cake Currency tool to quantify intangible factors, aiming to shift from No/Maybe to Yes.

The Cross Innovation Pre-Meeting
Microplastics are a challenge that affects us and other species enormously. We’re gathering a group of 15 experts in plastics and microplastics and/or waterways who collectively will map out various concrete challenges that we can only tackle together.
