Watch presentations from the main stage at Impact Innovation Summit

The summit provided a plethora of real-world impact stories from many different speakers, along with practical and active sessions where the participants got hands on tools and methods for transformation. The speakers shared inspiring examples of how they have worked to create impact, in everything from autonomous boats and seaweed cultivation to climate neutrality and rehabilitation in the Ukraine. Below, you can watch the speakers presenting at the main stage. The grand opening

The future is shaped by those who imagine beyond the present

Carina Leffler, director of the city of Helsingborg, opened the summit along with Aliya Sabir, head of sustainability and startups at Hetch. Carina emphasised how the summit is a catalyst for bold, impactful partnerships – to tackle welfare and climate challenges.

After the opening statement, the headline speakers of the summit entered the stage.

Daria Krivonos spoke about the importance of embracing multiple futures and stressed the importance of foresight and flexibility in an unpredictable world where change is constant. Thomas Osdoba talked about the need for cities to embrace strategic innovation and multi-level governance to achieve climate neutrality and resilience. Continuing on the topic of sustainability, Brita Staal emphasised that sustainability should be seen not as a distant goal but as an immediate and concrete responsibility for everyone, from individuals to companies. As the last headline speaker, Niklas Madsen gave the audience insights on how to embrace experimentation and risk-taking, with a focus on process over predetermined outcomes.

The toolbox

Ignite your path to impact

The afternoon of the second summit day was filled with hands-on sessions. Learning from talented leaders, there was a wide array of insights and tangible tools to bring home.

On the main stage, Erik Fernholm guided participants through The Inner Development Goals, ending with thought provoking and challenging exercises.

Next Agents hosted two sessions, one on Rapid Learning Cycles and one on the Sustainability-Driven Innovation Matrix. Rapid Learning Cycles can support both innovation and sustainability by enabling more agile decision-making, while the Sustainability-Driven Innovation Matrix connects sustainability efforts with business innovation, aiming to align organizational goals across both.

In parallel, two sessions on foresight were held on one of the stages. Fredrika Yngwe from Planethon held a session on how future foresight can have significant impact on decision-making and sustainability. The other session, hosted by Karin Lycke, Sara Tunheden and Max Larcombe, emphasized the role of leaders in shaping preferable futures through innovative foresight tools.

The summit ended with a collective wrap-up session, led by Fernanda Torre, and Louise Callenberg, where all participants reflected on their learnings from the summit before, creating concrete actions to take during the next 3 months to work towards their individual goals. In short, what impact can we start to create – now and in the years to come.
