International School of Helsingborg

Education for a better world

We believe learning has no boundaries and welcome students from ages 2 to 19 into a truly international community, offering a world-class education. Our International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme opens doors to top universities in Sweden and around the world – giving students the best possible start to their future.

Diploma Programme (Gymnasieprogram)

Launch your journey to the world’s top universities with our three year IB track. Year 1 creates academic foundations, and year 2-3 immerses you in the prestigious IB Diploma Programme. Here you’ll evolve into a curious, critical thinker with a global outlook, ready for what comes next.

Read more about the Diploma Programme

Middle Years Programme

The Middle Years Programme (MYP) is a programme of study designed to meet the particular educational needs of students between 11 and 16 years. It is internationally accredited and prepares students over a period of four or five years for entry into the IB Diploma programme.

Read more about the Middle Years Programme

Primary Years Programme

The PYP (including Early Years) is designed for students from the year they turn 3 to 12 years of age. It focuses on the development of the whole child as an inquirer, both in the classroom and in the world outside.

It is a framework guided by six interdisciplinary themes of global significance, explored using knowledge and skills derived from six subjects areas, as well as interdisciplinary skills, with a powerful emphasis on inquiry.

Read more about the Primary Years Programme

IB Continuum

Today, International School of Helsingborg have approximately 825 students between 2 and 19 years of age attending our Pre-Early Years, Early Years, Primary School, Middle School and Diploma Programme with International Baccalaureate.
